The DioQuest process

Why waste when you can recycle?

Our process takes point-source CO2 emissions and CO2 from direct air capture, reacts them with produced water, and permanently embeds the carbon in products that are high in performance and value.

Key benefits

One process, four ways to turn waste into value.

01

Produced water re-use

At the end of our processing, the produced water from oil & gas operations or from geothermal brines is ready for reuse as irrigation or in desalination.

02

CO2 capture

Our DioQuest process permanently captures CO2. We take point-source CO2 emissions or CO2 from direct air capture and embed them into our products, which contain as much as 25%–35% CO2 by mass.

03

Mining minerals

Our methodology mines high-value minerals from produced water, including calcium, magnesium, potassium, lithium and strontium. Once extracted, they can be used in high-value applications and the water can be more economically processed.

04

High-performance materials

Our CO2-negative materials provide a green alternative to current market offerings while also exceeding the performance of our competitors. We are developing high-strength facades and panels, and high-performance fillers for plastics.
