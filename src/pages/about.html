Who we are

Built to realize the potential of carbon conversion.

DioQuest™, a Semplastics Company, was founded in 2021 in order to fully realize the potential of our unique, patent-pending carbon conversion and utilization technology.

Our team

Decades of materials science, pointed at carbon.

Bill Easter

CEO

With a lifelong passion for materials and science, Bill has founded numerous companies to explore the untapped potential of high-performance materials technology.

As an accomplished entrepreneur and engineer, Bill has a geology degree from the University of Texas, a degree in Chemical Engineering from Drexel, and a Master's degree in Engineering Sciences from Penn State. Before going into business himself, he worked at AT&T and Bell Labs in microelectronics.

Dr. Walt Sherwood

Chief Scientist

Dr. Sherwood has over 32 years of polymer design and development experience, with over 20 years of polymer manufacturing and scale-up experience. He holds more than 14 U.S. patents covering the development of pre-ceramic polymers and high-temperature resins, and pioneered routes to the low-cost, high-yield, ceramic-forming polymers utilized by DioQuest.

Dr. Sherwood has a Ph.D. in Material Science and Engineering and a B.S. in Physics from RPI.

Dr. Brian Mueller

Technology Director

Dr. Mueller is a physical inorganic chemist who studied CO2 activation and fixation for his dissertation. He has been involved in research and regulations for the beneficial reuse of produced water in New Mexico. He brings a unique combination of physical inorganic chemistry, water treatment expertise, and material science skills.

Dr. Mueller has a Ph.D. in Chemistry from Texas A&M, a Post-Doc Fellowship in Materials Science and Engineering from the University of Michigan, an MBA from Kellstadt School of Business at DePaul University and a B.A. in Chemistry from Gustavus Adolphus College.

“Our ability to take multiple waste streams and create sustainable, carbon negative materials is a game-changer and will result in large volumes of CO2 being permanently locked away.”

Bill Easter, CEO

A Semplastics company

Part of a family that turns waste into materials.

Over the last decade, Semplastics and its subsidiary companies have pioneered new technology that has led to waste materials being re-used for building materials, recycled batteries and more.

X-MAT & X-BATT

DioQuest is a member of the Semplastics portfolio of companies, alongside X-MAT and X-BATT.

$14 million+

in grants and contracts secured by these companies for research and development of their technologies, including uses for waste streams such as CO2, recycled graphite, coal fines, fly ash and produced waters.

UCF Incubation Program

DioQuest is part of the UCF Incubation Program, with access to equipment that allows the company to expand carbon conversion research and development.
